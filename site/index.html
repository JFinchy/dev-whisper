New in 0.3

Made for talking to coding agents.

Claude Code, Cursor and the terminal are where most of your words go now. These features are for them.

  • Knows your codebase. Names come from the repo you're working in, so "the paste text function in the paste file" comes out as paste_text in @src-tauri/src/paste.rs.
  • Transcribes while you talk. Each phrase is transcribed at the pause after it, so long pauses cost nothing and the text is ready about a quarter of a second after you stop.
  • "Send it." "Attach the error." "Scratch that." Submit the prompt, add your latest stack trace as a code block, or take back the last paste.
  • Reply to a waiting agent. A Claude Code hook tells Dev Whisper when an agent is waiting. Press ⌃⌥R, answer out loud, and the reply lands in that agent's window while you stay where you were.
  • Learns from your corrections. History keeps what you said next to the cleaned-up text. Fix the same word twice and it offers to remember it.

Everything between the key press and the paste.

Fits where you're typing

Knows the app you're in
Sentences in Slack and email. File tags and real line breaks in Claude Code, Cursor and your terminal. Set your own mode for any app.
Two-stage progress you can see
An amber bar while it transcribes, a violet one while the LLM tidies up, each with a real percentage.
Paste it as-is
Click the violet bar, or add Shift to your hotkey, and the transcript pastes without waiting for the LLM.

Speaks developer

Your vocabulary
An editable list of the terms you use, so kubectl and Hammerspoon come out right. The LLM sees it too, so it won't "fix" them.
Casing on command
Say "snake case", "camel case", "kebab case", "Pascal case" or "screaming snake case" before a name.
Backtrack, lists, punctuation
"Actually" and "scratch that" correct you mid-sentence. Spoken lists become numbered lists. Say "period" or "new line" when you want them.
Snippets and boilerplate
A short cue pastes a saved block of text. "Generate boilerplate for…" writes the code.

Stays out of your way

Only refines when it helps
Clean sentences skip the LLM entirely. Fillers, stutters and casual chat get a quick pass.
Hands-free if you want it
Say "Dev Whisper, …" and only your enrolled voice triggers it. It pauses itself during calls.
Scriptable
open devwhisper://start-recording from any tool, plus a webhook for each finished transcript.
History and insights
Search every past dictation, re-run one through a different mode, and see your speaking speed.

Your voice never leaves your Mac.

Get Dev Whisper

Free. For Macs with Apple Silicon, macOS 12 or later.

Download Dev-Whisper.dmg

Latest version · All releases · Updates install themselves.

  1. Open the DMG and drag Dev Whisper into Applications.
  2. Right-click the app and choose Open the first time. It isn't notarized by Apple yet, so a plain double-click is blocked once.
  3. Follow the setup checklist in Settings: download a speech model (Base, 57 MB, is a good start) and allow Accessibility so it can paste.
  4. Optional: install Ollama for LLM cleanup, then pick a small model in Settings → LLM.
